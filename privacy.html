Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026 | Kopi Kia ("we", "our", or "us")
Summary: Kopi Kia is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data from users. This policy covers Kopi Kia on the web, on the Apple App Store and on Google Play.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Kopi Kia does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, device identifiers, or precise location data.

2. Local Data Storage

All game state data — including campaign level progress, unlocked recipes, virtual career earnings, high scores, and sound preferences — is stored strictly on your local device via local browser storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / IndexedDB). This data remains on your device and is never sent to external servers.

3. Analytics & Third-Party Services

Kopi Kia does not use third-party advertising networks, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics SDKs, or user profiling tools.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, Kopi Kia complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play's Families policy regarding children's privacy.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
