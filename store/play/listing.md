## Full description (max 4000)
```
Step behind the counter of a 1950s Nanyang kopitiam and learn the art of Singapore hawker coffee.

Uncle Lim wants his Kopi. Mdm Tan is waiting on a Teh-C. The queue is growing and the shift clock is ticking. Can you keep up?

BREW THE REAL THING
Pull kopi from the sock, steep the teh, and finish it the way the auntie asked for it. Condensed milk, evaporated milk, sugar, ice — every order is a different combination, and every combination has a name a real kopitiam regular would recognise.

Kopi, Kopi-O, Kopi-C, Kopi-C Siew Dai, Kopi Kosong, Kopi Peng, Teh, Teh-O, Teh-C, Teh Peng and Yuan Yang — plus Iced Bandung, Iced Lime Juice, and the cold cans in the ice bucket: Coke, Sprite and 100 Plus.

SERVE THE REGULARS
Uncle Lim, Mdm Tan, Aunty Lee, Brother Ah Seng and Student Kenneth each have their own order and their own patience. Learn what they drink.

A CAMPAIGN ACROSS SINGAPORE
Ten shifts across hawker centres you know: Tiong Bahru, Maxwell, Old Airport Road, Amoy Street, Chinatown Complex, Lau Pa Sat, Tekka Market, Geylang Serai, Adam Road and Changi Village. Each shift raises the target and shortens your patience. Earn stars, bank your takings, and spend it on better gear — a faster strainer, a gourmet roast, a turbo ice crusher, a golden mug.

MADE WITH CARE
- A hand-drawn vintage Nanyang aesthetic, from the poster art to the Milkmaid tins
- A real 3D glass mug that fills, layers and sloshes as you pour
- Sound built for the setting — the clatter of a busy food court behind every order

RESPECTS YOUR TIME AND YOUR PRIVACY
No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No tracking.
Your progress is saved on your own device and never leaves it.
Plays completely offline — on the MRT, on a plane, anywhere.

Also playable free in any browser at https://kopitiam.lol

Kopi Kia is a love letter to Singapore coffee culture. Shiok!
```
